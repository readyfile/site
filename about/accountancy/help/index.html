Help & Support

Find answers to common questions about getting started, billing, and using Readyfile.

How to sign up?

  1. Go to app.readyfile.co
  2. Select "Sign Up"
  3. Fill in all your details
  4. Follow the instructions to complete setup and instantly log in

How can I claim free trial credit?

If you are eligible, email us at support@readyfile.co from the email address you registered with, and we will issue £15 free credit within one working day.

Note: Free trial eligibility is offered exclusively to new customers and is granted at the sole discretion of Readyfile Ltd.

How do I top up?

Log into your account and navigate to the "Billing" portal. From there, you can choose either option:

  • Follow the step-by-step instructions to Pay by Bank
  • Scroll down to select and complete payment via Pay by Card

What is my billing tariff?

All current fees are accessible inside your "Billing" portal. As of 07/08/2026, default pricing is structured as follows:

Service Item Rate
Scanned Invoice 2p per page
Scanned Bank Statement 3p per page
Drive Storage 12p per GB-month
Monthly Maintenance Fee £0.00 (Free)

If you process high volumes, customized volume rates are available. Contact us at support@readyfile.co or use the "Contact" page as you scroll to the bottom of your logged in app.

Do I have any account limits?

Yes. By default, accounts are permitted a limit of 50 clients. This limit can easily be increased upon request.

If you require a higher client capacity, please email us at support@readyfile.co or use the "Contact" page as you scroll to the bottom of your logged in app.

How does it work?

The app allows you to:

  • Upload invoices and bank statements to an individual client — you can create one with a reference and an alias
  • View individual invoices and statements uploaded in the "Drive", including editing any errors which may arise from unreadable invoices
  • Generate a "Report", consolidating bank statements with invoice uploads

Do you have another query?

You can email us at support@readyfile.co, or use the "Contact" page as you scroll to the bottom of your logged in app.
