1. Definitions

In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

  • "Almonzo" means the charity management and software platform owned and operated by Readyfile Ltd, accessible at almonzo.org.
  • "Company Websites" refers collectively to readyfile.co, almonzo.org, get.almonzo.org, and their respective subdomains.
  • "Affiliate Link" means a unique tracking URL, snippet, or referral code issued to the Partner via get.almonzo.org or designated administrative portals.
  • "New Customer" means an eligible UK charity or approved not-for-profit organisation that has not held an active or trial subscription for Almonzo within the preceding twenty-four (24) months.
  • "Programme" means the Almonzo Partner, Channel, and Affiliate Programme managed by Readyfile Ltd.

2. Non-Exclusivity

This Agreement is non-exclusive. Participation in the Programme does not restrict Readyfile Ltd from appointing additional partners, affiliates, or resellers, nor does it restrict the Partner from promoting other products, provided doing so does not breach the intellectual property, non-disparagement, or confidentiality obligations herein.

3. Partner Acceptance & Vetting

Acceptance into the Programme is subject to explicit approval at the sole discretion of Readyfile Ltd. We reserve the right to review, audit, reject, or suspend any Partner application or existing account at any time for non-compliance, reputational risk, or security concerns.

4. Customer Transactions & Commission Scope

Commissions are earned strictly on a New Customer's initial qualifying paid transaction resulting directly from a verified Affiliate Link trackable via get.almonzo.org or readyfile.co.

Scope Limitation: Unless explicitly agreed in a separate written addendum signed by Readyfile Ltd, no commission is payable on subscription renewals, plan upgrades, additional user seats, custom add-ons, implementation fees, or subsequent purchases made beyond the initial qualifying transaction.

5. Commission Eligibility & Thresholds

A commission payment shall only become due and payable if all of the following conditions are satisfied:

  1. The lead is valid, verified, and uniquely tracked to the Partner via an authorized Affiliate Link on get.almonzo.org or almonzo.org.
  2. A qualifying paid customer transaction is completed and fully settled in cleared funds.
  3. The referred customer is an eligible UK charity registered with the Charity Commission (or OSCR/CCNI) or an approved UK not-for-profit organisation.
  4. The referred customer has paid a net total (excluding VAT, discounts, credits, and refunds) equal to at least 125% of the commission amount due.
  5. The customer account remains active and in good standing without dispute or cancellation throughout the required verification period.

6. Self-Referrals & Related-Party Restrictions

No commission shall be earned or paid for self-referrals. This includes referrals originating from:

  • The Partner's own personal or business entities.
  • Parent, subsidiary, affiliate, or commonly controlled corporate entities.
  • Officers, directors, employees, or immediate family members of the Partner.

Attempted self-referral constitutes a material breach and results in immediate forfeiture of all accumulated commissions and termination of the Partner account.

7. Payment Terms & Schedule

Commissions are calculated in UK Sterling (GBP £) and reconciled on a monthly basis.

  • Minimum Payout Threshold: Payments are processed only when accumulated cleared commissions reach or exceed £50. Balances below £50 roll over to the subsequent processing cycle.
  • Verification Hold: Commissions are held for a minimum of 30 days following customer payment to ensure the transaction is clear of refunds or chargeback requests.
  • Disbursement Method: Payments are issued via UK bank transfer to the Partner bank account provided during registration on get.almonzo.org.

8. Refunds, Adjustments & Chargebacks

Readyfile Ltd reserves the right to reclaim, offset, or deduct previously paid or pending commissions if the underlying customer transaction is refunded, reversed, cancelled, subject to a credit note, or subject to a credit card chargeback. Any negative commission balance resulting from adjustments may be offset against future earnings.

9. Marketing, PPC & Brand Guidelines

Partners must maintain high ethical marketing standards. The Partner agrees to strictly adhere to the following rules:

  • No Search Engine Bidding (PPC): Partners must not bid on search engine keywords or trade marks containing "Almonzo", "Readyfile", or close variations/misspellings thereof on platforms such as Google Ads, Bing Ads, or social media networks.
  • Domain Restrictions: Partners may not register or use domain names, subdomains, or social media handles containing the terms "Almonzo" or "Readyfile".
  • No Spam or Deceptive Tactics: Unsolicited bulk emails (spam), misleading claims, or deceptive advertising tactics are strictly prohibited.
  • Transparency: Partners must clearly disclose their affiliate relationship with Almonzo in accordance with applicable Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) and Consumer Protection regulations.

10. Data Protection & GDPR Compliance

Each party agrees to comply with all applicable UK Data Protection Legislation (including the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018). Partners must not capture or store end-customer financial details or personally identifiable information on behalf of Readyfile Ltd without explicit authorization. Referrals must be routed directly via designated links to almonzo.org or readyfile.co.

14. Term & Termination

  • Immediate Termination: Readyfile Ltd reserves the right to terminate this Agreement immediately without notice in the event of fraud, insolvency, material breach of marketing rules, reputational harm, or unauthorized IP usage by the Partner.
  • Effect of Termination: Upon termination, all licences grant immediately cease, Affiliate Links must be removed, and unpaid commissions for non-qualifying leads shall be voided.
