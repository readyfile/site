3. Accepted Payment Methods

You may pay for services on Readyfile using any of the following approved payment methods:

  1. Credit / Debit Card: We accept all major cards, including at minimum Visa and Mastercard. Card payments are processed immediately through secure third-party gateways.
  2. Bank Transfer: Bank transfers are accepted provided you are sending funds to a GBP (£) bank account maintained in the United Kingdom.
    Important: All bank transfers must include the pre-specified 5-character reference provided during the checkout or top-up process. Failure to include this reference may delay the crediting of your account.

4. Pre-Payment, Account Credit & Usage

  • Advance Top-Up Model: Services are billed on a pre-paid basis. Payments must be made in advance to top up your account balance before consuming services.
  • Service Pricing & Deduction: Specific pricing for each service on our platform is clearly displayed at app.readyfile.co. Funds will be deducted directly from your available balance as you use the services.
  • Credit Expiration: Pre-paid account credit does not expire, provided Readyfile Ltd remains solvent.

5. Refunds & Administration Fees

  • Non-Refundable Credit: As a general rule, purchased credit added to your account is non-refundable once uploaded.
  • 15-Day Account Opening Exception: You may request a refund of unconsumed credit strictly within 15 calendar days of opening your account.
  • Administration Fee: Any approved refund request made within the 15-day window is subject to an administration and processing fee of up to 5%, which will be deducted from the refunded total.

6. Taxes & Currency

  • Currency: All prices, transactions, and account balances are denominated and payable in British Pounds Sterling (GBP £).
  • Taxes: Unless explicitly stated otherwise, prices quoted on the platform may be exclusive of applicable taxes (such as UK Value Added Tax - VAT). Applicable taxes will be calculated and added during payment processing based on your billing location.

7. Payment Security & Data Protection

We prioritize the security of your financial data:

  • All credit and debit card transactions are handled by PCI-DSS compliant payment processing partners.
  • Readyfile Ltd does not directly store, process, or retain complete raw payment card numbers on our servers.
  • All transmission of payment data is encrypted using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL) technology.

8. Billing Disputes & Failed Payments

  • Failed Transactions: If a payment fails or is rejected by your financial institution, services will not be rendered or topped up until successful settlement occurs.
  • Disputes & Chargebacks: If you believe a payment error has occurred, please contact us immediately before filing a chargeback or dispute with your bank or card issuer. Unreasonable chargebacks may result in immediate suspension or termination of your account balance and access.
