3. Payment Methods & SCA Security

Payments for account credits and services on readyfile.co can be made via:

  1. Credit & Debit Cards: We accept major debit and credit cards (Visa and Mastercard). In compliance with UK and EU Payment Services Regulations (PSD2 / Strong Customer Authentication), card payments undergo 3D Secure verification where required by your issuing bank.
  2. Bank Transfers (Pay by Bank): Bank transfers are accepted for UK GBP (£) transactions.
    Important: You must include your allocated unique 5-character reference on every bank transfer. Omission of this reference will cause processing delays or failure of automatic credit assignment to your account at app.readyfile.co.

4. Pre-Payment, Usage & Tariff Deduction

  • Pre-Paid Credit Model: Services are billed on a pay-as-you-go, advance credit basis. Account credit must be purchased before processing documents or utilizing premium features.
  • Tariff Rates: Current document processing and storage rates are maintained in real-time within your app.readyfile.co Billing Portal and outlined on our main site at readyfile.co.
  • Automatic Deductions: Funds are automatically deducted from your available credit upon consumption of services (e.g., invoice/bank statement extraction, storage utilization).
  • Credit Expiration: Account credit balance does not expire while Readyfile Ltd remains active and solvent.

Readyfile Ltd primarily serves business clients and commercial entities (B2B). Where services are accessed by consumers (B2C), statutory regulations apply as follows:

  • 15-Day New Account Credit Refund: As a commercial courtesy, you may request a refund of remaining, unconsumed pre-paid credit within 15 calendar days of opening your initial account.
  • Administration Fee: Approved refunds of unconsumed credits requested within the 15-day window are subject to an administrative and payment processing fee of up to 5% to cover non-recoverable payment gateway charges.
  • Discretionary Refunds: After 15 days, or for partial usage, any refund or balance return is granted strictly at the sole discretion of Readyfile Ltd. Free trial credits or promotional bonuses are entirely non-refundable and non-transferable.

Your payment information is secured according to rigorous industry standards:
