3. Data Controller vs. Data Processor Roles

Under applicable UK Data Protection Legislation (including the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018), legal roles are split as follows:

  • Readyfile as Data Controller: We act as the Data Controller for your business account data (e.g., your registration details, billing contact info, and transaction records required to provide and manage your account).
  • Readyfile as Data Processor: We act strictly as a Data Processor regarding any client data, end-customer documents, bank statements, or client records that you upload, enter, or upload into our applications.
    As the Data Controller for your clients' data, you are responsible for ensuring you have a lawful basis to collect and process their information, and you must supply your clients with your own privacy policy.

4. Data We Collect: Account Data vs. Client Data

A. Your Account & Business Data (Readyfile as Data Controller)

We collect minimal business information necessary to operate your account and render services:

  • Business name and physical business address
  • Account contact names
  • Business email addresses
  • Billing transaction history and invoices

Note on Payment Cards: Financial payment details (e.g., raw credit/debit card numbers) are collected and processed directly by our PCI-compliant payment partners. Readyfile does not store raw card numbers on its servers.

B. Client Data Uploaded by You (Readyfile as Data Processor)

To provide automated bookkeeping and accountancy features, you may supply us with data originating from your clients (e.g., scanned bank statements, receipts, and invoices). This data is kept strictly separate from core platform user data and is processed solely according to your specific system instructions.

5. Artificial Intelligence & OpenAI Processing

We utilize advanced Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology to automate data extraction from uploaded documents (such as scanned bank statements, invoices, and financial receipts).

AI Infrastructure & Data Privacy Commitment:
  • Provider: Our automated document recognition and extraction capabilities are powered by OpenAI APIs.
  • Opted Out of Model Training: All client data and documents processed via OpenAI APIs are strictly opted out of AI model training. Your data and your clients' data are never used by OpenAI or Readyfile to train, fine-tune, or improve AI models.
  • Purpose Limitation: Data sent to OpenAI is transmitted securely and processed exclusively for the transient purpose of extracting text and financial figures needed for your accountancy workflows.

6. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion Exceptions

  • Server Infrastructure: All data collected, processed, and hosted by Readyfile is stored on secure servers located physically in London, United Kingdom.
  • Mandatory 10-Year Invoice Retention: We keep a record of all billing invoices for 10 years from the date of the payment transaction to comply with legal, tax, and accounting statutory obligations.
  • Data Deletion Rights & Exceptions: Upon request, we will delete all account and personal data held in our systems, except for invoice data (which includes business addresses and billing records), which must be retained for the full 10-year statutory period.

7. Payments Policy

  1. Accepted Payment Methods:
    • Debit & Credit Cards: We accept Visa and Mastercard at minimum. Card payments are processed immediately through secure third-party gateways.
    • Bank Transfers: Accepted if sent to a GBP (£) bank account based in the United Kingdom.
    Mandatory Transfer Reference: All bank transfers must include the pre-specified 5-character reference provided during setup or checkout. Bank transfers missing this 5-character reference may face processing delays.
  2. Pre-Payment & Account Top-Ups: Payments are made in advance as a balance top-up prior to consuming platform services. Pricing for individual services is specified at app.readyfile.co, and funds are deducted directly from your account balance upon service usage.
  3. Credit Expiration: Purchased top-up credits do not expire while Readyfile Ltd remains solvent.
  4. Refunds & Fees: Purchased credit is generally non-refundable. However, you may request a refund within 15 calendar days of opening your account. Approved refunds requested within this period are subject to an administrative fee of up to 5%.
